Monthly strategy sounds good but often creates distance from execution.

## Weekly intent keeps teams aligned

A good weekly process should answer three questions:

1. What matters this week?
2. What can block us?
3. What will we finish by Friday?

## Keep it short and concrete

If your weekly meeting cannot fit in 20 minutes, your scope is probably too broad.

## What to document

- one weekly goal
- three must-do tasks
- one risk owner

That is enough structure to stay sharp.
