A sustainable rhythm is more valuable than occasional motivation spikes.

## A simple cadence

- Daily: one key outcome
- Weekly: one version checkpoint
- Monthly: one system improvement

## Why this works

You reduce context switching and create visible progress every week.

## What to avoid

Do not over-optimize tooling before your workflow is stable.

Process clarity first, tooling second.
