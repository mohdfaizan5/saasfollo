Teams are moving from high-volume output to high-leverage systems.

## Trend 1: Decision latency is the new bottleneck

The fastest firms are not doing more work. They are making decisions faster with clear context.

## Trend 2: Delivery quality is becoming productized

The best teams define repeatable quality bars instead of relying on heroic effort.

## Trend 3: Visibility is now a client expectation

Clients increasingly expect transparent progress, not just final delivery.

Founders who build visibility into weekly workflows create stronger trust and better retention.
