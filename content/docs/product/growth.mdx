## The Problem

Solo founders build, build, build — and forget to market. There's always one more feature to ship. Meanwhile, SEO pages don't get written, cold DMs don't get sent, and Reddit posts don't get published. Marketing feels like a chore because there's no system for it.

## What Growth Does

Growth turns marketing into a **sprint** — tied to your active version. You pick your channels, set targets, and track daily progress. It's your growth accountability partner.

## How It Works

**3-Step Setup:**

1. **Pick Channels** — Choose from 13 activity types: SEO Pages, Cold DMs, Cold Emails, Paid Ads, A/B Testing, Analytics, User Feedback, CRO, Accelerator Apps, Reddit Posts, Events Attended, and Custom Activity
2. **Set Targets** — For each channel, set a numeric goal (e.g., "Write 20 SEO pages by deadline"). Pick a sprint deadline
3. **Review & Start** — See a summary with the calculated **daily pace** for each activity (target ÷ remaining days). Hit "Start Sprint"

**Then, every day:**

- Open Growth, log what you did (activity + quantity + optional note)
- Watch your **streak** grow — consecutive days with at least one growth log
- See per-activity progress bars fill up
- A radial gauge shows overall sprint completion

## Key Capabilities

- **Streak Counter** — Days in a row you've logged at least one growth activity. Miss a day, it resets. Simple, effective pressure
- **Daily Pace** — Auto-calculated targets per day so you know if you're ahead or behind
- **Per-Activity Progress** — Visual bars with hatched patterns showing completed vs target, with percentage badges
- **Sprint Deadline** — Countdown in days. Growth is time-boxed, just like your version
- **Log Dialog** — Quick entry: pick activity, enter count, add a note. Done in 5 seconds
- **Version-Scoped** — Each growth sprint belongs to a version. New version, new sprint

## Why It Matters

Building a product without marketing is like opening a restaurant with no sign on the door. Growth makes the non-product work **visible, measurable, and habitual**. The streak mechanic alone is enough to keep you showing up.
