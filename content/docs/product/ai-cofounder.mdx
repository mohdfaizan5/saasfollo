## The Problem

Solo founders make every decision alone — architecture, pricing, copy, priorities. You can't afford a cofounder or a consultant for every question. And generic AI tools (ChatGPT, etc.) don't know your project. You end up copy-pasting context before every question.

## What AI Cofounder Does

AI Cofounder is a **context-aware chat assistant** that already knows your project — tasks, versions, progress, everything. Ask it a question and it responds with your actual data, not generic advice.

Powered by Claude Sonnet 4.5 with streaming responses and visual data cards.

## Key Capabilities

- **6 Expert Personas** — Switch who you're talking to:
  - **Developer** — Concrete implementation steps, edge cases, next task to ship
  - **CTO** — Architecture, risk assessment, delivery sequencing
  - **SEO Expert** — Rankings, keywords, page health, next SEO moves
  - **Copywriter** — Benefit-led copy, microcopy, funnel-stage tuning
  - **Customer** — Usability audit, job-to-be-done validation, honest feedback
  - **Content Creator** — Outlines, hooks, audience pain-points
- **Tool Calling with Visual Cards** — When you ask about tasks or versions, the AI calls live tools and renders rich UI cards:
  - **Tasks Card** — Progress bar, status icons, priority badges, scrollable task list
  - **Versions Card** — Collapsible version sections, per-version progress, global completion gauge
- **Quick Chat Mode** — Toggle for ultra-short 1–3 sentence answers. No filler, just bullets and direct answers
- **Streaming Markdown** — Responses render as formatted markdown with syntax-highlighted code blocks, streamed in real-time
- **Suggestion Cards** — On empty state, three starter prompts: "Build my roadmap", "Set priorities", "Polish my pitch"

## How It Works

1. Open AI Cofounder from the sidebar
2. Pick a persona (defaults to Developer)
3. Type a question or click a suggestion card
4. The AI reads your project context (tasks, versions, progress), calls tools if needed, and streams a response
5. Tool results appear as interactive cards inline with the conversation

## Why It Matters

You're not alone. At 2 AM when you're deciding whether to ship the auth flow or the billing page first, AI Cofounder has your task list, your version deadlines, and a CTO persona ready to tell you which one matters more.

Full project context. Zero copy-pasting. Available on every decision.
