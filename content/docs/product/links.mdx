## The Problem

Every SaaS project uses 15–30 external tools — GitHub, Vercel, Stripe, Figma, Supabase, PostHog, Sentry, Google Analytics. These live in browser bookmarks, Notion tables, and Slack messages. Finding the right dashboard at the right moment wastes minutes every day.

## What Links Does

Links is a **visual bookshelf** for every URL related to your project. Paste a link, and it auto-detects the service, applies the right icon and brand color, and puts it on a card.

## Key Capabilities

- **Auto-Detection** — Recognizes 100+ services and applies branded styling:
  - **Dev:** GitHub, GitLab, Vercel, Netlify, Railway, Docker, Postman
  - **Design:** Figma, Framer, Webflow, Miro, Dribbble, Excalidraw
  - **Data:** Supabase, Firebase, MongoDB, PostgreSQL, Redis, PlanetScale
  - **Analytics:** PostHog, Mixpanel, Hotjar, Plausible, Google Analytics
  - **Payments:** Stripe, Paddle, LemonSqueezy, Gumroad
  - **Social:** Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Reddit, Product Hunt, YouTube
  - **Any other URL** — Falls back to favicon detection with a neutral card
- **Bulk Paste** — Paste multiple URLs at once (one per line). All get created and auto-detected
- **Tag System** — Organize links by tags (e.g., "Frontend", "Analytics", "Marketing"). Tags auto-suggest from existing ones with fuzzy matching. Grid groups links by tag
- **Bento Grid Layout** — Links display as rounded cards with brand-colored backgrounds, icons, labels, and domain names
- **Hover Actions** — Edit, delete, and open-in-new-tab buttons appear on hover. Clean by default, functional when needed
- **Edit Dialog** — Change the URL or tag with autocomplete suggestions

## Why It Matters

One click to any tool in your stack. No bookmark hunting, no Notion digging. Everything visual, everything grouped, everything inside the project where it belongs.
