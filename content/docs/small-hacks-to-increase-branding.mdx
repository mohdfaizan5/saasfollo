Things to do:
1. Add a favicon to your website. This small icon that appears in the browser tab can help increase brand recognition and make your site look more professional.
2. Use consistent colors and fonts across your website and marketing materials. This helps create a cohesive
brand identity and makes it easier for customers to recognize your brand.
3. Create a memorable logo that represents your brand. A well-designed logo can help increase brand recognition and make your business more memorable to customers.
4. make smooth, simple, easy, memorable and useful onboarding process that should make customers,subconsiously thye'll feel wow they've asked me the right questions, maybe I'm getting a better value inside. This sets the expectations high of the user which compounds them to put/spend extra energy to take a proper look of the soft (here they'll feel this is THE app, i been looking for, now I just have to learn to use it to it's fullest; by this you reduce the churn rates and make users fully understand your product, increasing the LTV) This can help create a positive first impression of your brand and increase customer loyalty.
5. Create high-quality content that provides value to your audience. This can help establish your brand as an authority in your industry and increase brand loyalty among customers.
6. if you've a blog, make sure to include your logo and consistent branding elements in your blog posts. This can help increase brand recognition and make your blog more memorable to readers.
7. Use branded packaging for your products. This can help increase brand recognition and make your products
stand out on store shelves.
8. create ways in which people share our content as they want to sound smart and well-informed. Then use smart ways, like attach a logo maybe in image like eraser.io or a ref=yoursite.link  This can help increase brand awareness and attract new customers to your business.
9. add share buttons on your blogs and content pages, making it easy for visitors to share your content on social media platforms. This can help increase brand visibility and attract new customers to your business.

10. use images and assets, this will make your content more visually appealing, less VANILA and  help increase brand recognition. Make sure to use high-quality images that are relevant to your brand and content.
