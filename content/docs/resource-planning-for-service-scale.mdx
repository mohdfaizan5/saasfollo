Growth does not break teams. Poor planning does.

## Capacity should be visible weekly

A weekly capacity view removes surprises. You can spot bottlenecks before they impact delivery.

## Plan around constraints, not optimism

Most teams plan with best-case assumptions. Better teams plan around real constraints:

- availability
- handoff delays
- review cycles

## A baseline planning rhythm

- Monday: capacity reset
- Wednesday: risk review
- Friday: scope adjustment

Small loops beat quarterly planning documents that nobody opens.
